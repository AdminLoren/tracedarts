Nickname      :   Meari Lou "Marilu" Hasashi
Bio           :   Gifted with sky-high intelligence practically from birth, Marilu navigates life with the unsettling mental maturity of someone decades older. 

Finding the typical antics of her actual four-year-old peers overwhelmingly dull and unappealing, she naturally gravitates toward the older friend group of her older brother instead. Armed with superior critical thinking skills and a sharp, passive-aggressive streak, she manages to be simultaneously biting and utterly adored—sharing a genuine, deep-seated affection with the group who happily keeps her around.

Marilu X Fumio
Relationship Type : Siblings
Title : My Loveable Idiot-Brother
Commentary : Even though Marilu frequently regards her older brother as quite dim-witted, she considers it her absolute duty to keep him grounded and fiercely loved. Marilu X Alicia
Relationship Type : Cousins
Title : Lowest IQ
Commentary : Just like with Fumio, Marilu genuinely views Alicia as a big sister, admiring her vibrant energy and raw capabilities. She holds deep affection for her, even if she constantly catches herself wishing big sister Alicia would actually put her brain to use every once in a while.

Marilu X Vyse
Relationship Type : Friends
Title : Unmoved by Charm
Commentary : Marilu has always held a quiet admiration for Vyse's soft, warm, and inviting aura, finding comfort in his gentle presence. However, her stubborn pride and sharp tongue mean she can never just compliment him outright—she always makes sure to remind him that his good looks will never fool her into thinking he's any different from the rest of the group.

Marilu X Riku.EXE
Relationship Type : Friends
Title : A New Standard
