10 YEARS AGO…

"Is that everything, dear?" Meari asked her husband as she took the contents out of her basket.

"Yeah, everything is accounted for..." Hanzo replied, placing the box of cutlet sandwiches on their table.

They had settled down on the school grounds for Family Day, an event where parents gathered to watch their children participate in sports and other activities. For now, the two were busy preparing the food they had brought for the occasion.

"That's all the lunches for the kids. Now, let's see..." Meari began counting the colorful heads of the little ones playing with one another.

She spotted a strawberry blonde, an indigo, a black-haired child, an auburn-haired one, and a navy blue-haired girl.

But someone was missing.

Maroon.

Her own son.

"...Where's Fumio? Hanzo, can you check where he is?" Meari asked, turning toward her husband.

Hanzo had overheard her question and immediately began scanning the crowd. His eyes moved from one colorful head to another, searching for his son's trademark maroon hair.

Eventually, he spotted him near the grassy area beside the relay track.

Fumio stood completely still, staring at the other tables in the distance.

"Fumio?" Hanzo approached his little one and knelt beside him.

[Hanzo] "What's wrong, buddy? You look... uncharacteristically quiet."

To his surprise, he was right.

Fumio had never been this quiet before, especially during an occasion where he could have fun, show off, and draw attention to himself. He absolutely loved being complimented by adults who thought he was adorable.

But right now?

His face was red as a tomato, his eyes fixed on something in the distance. He looked so flustered that Hanzo could practically imagine steam escaping from his son's head.

"U-Uh..." Hanzo stammered, unsure how to react to this unexpected discovery. "You look like you saw something..."

[Fumio] "...Hah—"

Fumio finally snapped out of his trance. He looked at his father before immediately denying the painfully obvious.

[Fumio] "No, I'm not! I'm just thinking about how I won that race for everyone!"

"Actually, it was Alicia who ran circles around everyone..." Hanzo deadpanned. He gently turned his son around by the shoulders until they were facing each other. "...It's a girl, isn't it?"

"Ha—Wha—?!" Fumio flinched in surprise. How had his dad figured it out so quickly?!

"You're blushing..." Hanzo pointed out with an affectionate smile. "That never happened before."

His expression was so warm and welcoming that Fumio found himself wanting to open up, even if just a little.

"...Welllll..." Fumio glanced away, his thoughts wandering back to his little crush. "...There's this girl... who I think looks, uh... pretty..." He paused. "B-But that's all you're getting!"

"Eh? That sounds like enough to me..." Hanzo chuckled before lifting Fumio by the underarms and carrying him.

[Hanzo] "You really like this girl, right? Then there shouldn't be anything stopping you from talking to her, except for yourself."

"...Me?" Fumio tilted his head, confused. "How can I stop myself?"

"By not doing anything about it." Hanzo looked up at the sky, his expression growing thoughtful as he reminisced about his own past.

[Hanzo] "Take it from me... I've missed out on a lot of fun stuff because I was too scared to try."

He looked back at his son. [Hanzo] "I don't want that happening to you, Fumio. I don't want you to grow up regretting all the opportunities you let slip away just because you were afraid to take them."

"...Oppor... toon-ety." Fumio struggled to pronounce the word, prompting a small laugh from his father.

"Hehe... So, can you promise me you'll be a little braver and talk to that girl you like?" Hanzo smiled. "There's no harm in trying, son."

"Hmmmm..." Fumio held his chin, putting on his thimccing cap as he considered his father's words. After a moment, he finally gave Hanzo his answer.

[Fumio] "...Okay! I'll talk to her next time I see her at school!"

Hanzo's face lit up with a gleeful grin. "That's what I like to hear..." He raised his fist, offering his son a fist bump. "Knock 'em dead for me, okay?"

"Hai! I will!" Fumio enthusiastically bumped his two tiny fists against his father's much larger one.

[Fumio] "I'll do it for you, Papa!"

PRESENT DAY

At last, the school day at Nijigasaki had come to an end.

Under the pleasant afternoon sky, students streamed through the school gates, eager to return home, collapse into their beds, or enjoy a satisfying meal. Some looked utterly exhausted, while others kept themselves occupied by chatting with their friends about whatever had happened during the day.

It was a chance for everyone to unwind after hours of classes.

Among them was a first-year student with hazy, maroon-red hair. He descended an escalator, watching the students around him walk and talk in pairs and groups. His expression remained neutral, as though the sight had become so familiar that it barely registered anymore.

His name was Fumio Hasashi.

[Fumio] "...No new faces. No new topics. Typical."

Fumio shrugged to himself, thoroughly unimpressed by his surroundings.

The young lad usually spent his time with his childhood friends, paying little attention to the other students his age. He was perfectly content with that arrangement, especially considering his current circumstances.

[Kyofú] "I have to agree with you," a voice spoke from within his mind, possessing enough sentience to hold a conversation of its own. [Kyofú] "I'd much rather listen to your ramblings than hear other people's. They never stop talking about the same things..."

"Yep. Welcome to my life." Fumio blew a raspberry as he stepped off the escalator. "No one's interesting nowadays..."

[Kyofú] "But seriously, kid. You might want to listen to your mom and meet some new people. I know it's hard to find anyone unique in this 'normie hellhole' you keep talking about, but maybe she was just trying to get your mind off the whole Satsujin situation and everything else you've been through."

"If anything, Kyofú, those experiences have only brought me closer to my friends and relatives..." Fumio made his way outside the building and decided to visit the river near the school.

[Fumio] "We survived a damn warzone..." He chuckled to himself. "Heh. How am I supposed to advertise that to other students? They wouldn't believe me."

[Kyofú] "Perhaps you're right. You do you. It's your body."

The voice fell silent, allowing Fumio to return to his aimless wandering. A little background music played through his headphones as he headed toward the river.

The water reflected the afternoon sunlight, creating beautiful patterns across its surface. When Fumio arrived at the grassy slope separating the river from the concrete walkway, he stopped to take in the scenery.

"You can really tell why Emma loves hanging out here..." Mumbling to himself, Fumio gently sat down on the grass near the riverbank and placed his bag beside him.

The atmosphere was comfortable, the surroundings peaceful. With his breathing slowing to a steady rhythm, he finally had a moment to reflect.

[Fumio] "...Mm. I wonder..."

He gazed at the sky before looking down at the water, where his reflection stared back at him.

[Fumio] "I wonder if this is what heaven feels like... Is it, Oto-san? I mean, it's pretty similar, isn't it? You get to rest after everything you've been through."

Fumio gave his reflection a gentle smile, one he rarely showed anyone else.

It reminded him of the same smile his late father used to wear. Seeing that familiar expression on his own face filled him with a quiet sense of pride.

People had always told him that he was the perfect combination of his parents' features. He had inherited his mother's beauty and his father's charisma, and apparently, the results had become rather noticeable as he grew older.

Who knew those genes would come in handy at this age?

Before he could dwell on the thought any further, his headphones beeped, signaling that their battery had run out.

[Fumio] "Ah, shit..." Annoyed by the sudden interruption, Fumio took them off.

Without music to accompany him, he continued enjoying the peaceful surroundings in silence. It was relaxing enough to help him forget the stress of another school day.

Then, he heard something.

A quiet sob.

[Fumio] "...That voice... It sounds like a girl."

Fumio turned toward the sound. After a moment's hesitation, he decided to investigate in case someone needed help.

The sobbing grew louder as he approached the opposite side of the riverbank. Unlike his side, this area had several small trees that provided plenty of shade over the grass.

There, beneath the shadows, sat a figure curled up with her knees drawn to her chest.

The darkness made it difficult to identify her, but one thing was apparent.

She was a girl.

[Fumio] "...That's a little creepy. What if it's a ghost trying to lure me in?"

Fumio squinted, attempting to determine whether he recognized the figure.

Unfortunately for him, he did.

[Fumio] "...OH GOD."

His body reacted before his brain could catch up. He dropped belly-first onto the grass as gently as he could, hoping the girl wouldn't notice him.

"What happened? Your heart rate just went up. Is it really a ghost?" Kyofú asked.

Fumio answered within his mind. "N-No! It's Sakura-san...!"

[Kyofú] "...Sakura? Sounds familiar. Is she an enemy?"

[Fumio] "N-No! Sh-She's... um..." Fumio couldn't finish the sentence. His face was already heating up.

[Kyofú] "Whatever she is, she looks like she's having a problem. Why don't you be a lover boy and help her out?"

"...I can't," Fumio squeaked.

[Kyofú] "That's quite rude of you. Usually, when you talk to girls, they freeze up when they see you. Now it's the other way around. And judging people by their looks is the real ugly thing to do."

"N-No, it's not like that! Besides, I'm not that ugly—" Fumio cautiously raised his head to check whether the girl was still there.

[Fumio] "I've been trying to talk to Sakura-san for so long that I eventually gave up..."

[Kyofú] "Seems like her feelings are the least of your concerns right now. Seriously, you've got to help her. You can't just lie there. Someone's going to see you."

"O-Of course I'll help! H-Hopefully, it'll... work out." Fumio attempted to stand, only to discover that his ankles weren't cooperating. He wobbled, struggling to keep himself upright.

Meanwhile, the distressed girl stared blankly at the flowing water before her. She was so consumed by her shame that she barely noticed Fumio's footsteps rustling through the grass.

[Fumiko] "...Nnf... I... I should've known... It was... useless. I don't even know how to face this anymore..."

The words were faint, but Fumio heard enough to make his heart skip a beat.

He hadn't expected the situation to be this serious.

That was all the motivation he needed.

[Fumio] "...H-Hey, miss?"

He hesitated, but eventually mustered the courage to do what he believed was right.

[Fumiko] "...!"

Fumiko Sakura's student council instincts kicked in immediately. She wiped away her tears and tried to clean up her ruined makeup before turning toward the approaching student as though nothing had happened.

[Fumiko] "Uu... Y-Yes? Do you need... do you need anything?"

Unfortunately, she hadn't managed to steady her voice in time.

Fumio responded with equal awkwardness.

[Fumio] "...Uh... N-No, no. Nothing at all..."

"...It's obvious what's going on with me, isn't it?" Dropping the act, Fumiko tucked her knees against her chest and began crying again. "If you have nothing else to say to me, I'd appreciate it if you left me alone..."

[Fumio] "...Ugh. Who am I kidding?"

Fumio wiped the sweat from his forehead and planted his trembling feet firmly on the ground, trying to shake off his anxiety. "It sounded like you were under a little bit of stress... May I ask what's bothering you?"

It took a moment, but Fumiko gently looked back at him, checking whether he had genuinely asked her that question.

"...Um... If it's a sensitive topic, I won't bother you." Fumio lowered his head, already preparing to leave.

[Fumiko] "No, no... Please..."

Fumio immediately turned back toward her, as though he had been magnetically pulled into place.

[Fumiko] "...I... I actually would really like to talk to someone about this."

"...Oh..." Fumio glanced toward the school to check whether anyone was watching before approaching the girl who needed someone to listen.

[Fumio] "Wouldn't someone you know be a better option?"

[Fumiko] "Shioriko and Nana-chan don't have time for me right now. They should focus on other things..."

Fumiko sniffled and uncrossed her legs, adjusting her skirt. "Sorry. I just really need to get this off my chest..."

"No, don't worry. You can tell me anything..." Fumio stood there awkwardly while Fumiko looked up at him. When their eyes met, he had absolutely no idea what to do next.

[Fumiko] "...So, are you just going to stand there, or...?"

Fumiko glanced at the patch of grass beside her, silently suggesting that he sit down.

"...O-Oh! O-Of course!" Fumio promptly sat beside her, moving so quickly that he failed to notice the little grin spreading across his face.

Fumiko, meanwhile, remained lost in thought, trying to gather her feelings into something she could explain.

"To put it simply... I don't usually want things. Not in the sense that I want them so badly that my life depends on them. But... I've really wanted this one for so long." She paused.

[Fumiko] "I wanted it to be mine."

[Fumio] "Oh. You didn't get something? Well, what was it?"

Fumiko fell silent for a moment.

Then, she finally answered. "...Someone's love."

...Ah.

Fumio's eyes widened.

He should've expected it, a girl wishing to be loved.

[Fumiko] "I've been caught up in responsibilities for so long that I haven't had much time to socialize. Sure, everybody pretty much knows me, but they don't know me personally..."

She lowered her gaze. [Fumiko] "So, I've really wanted someone to spend my free time with. Someone I could trust."

"...Ah..." Fumio nodded slowly, his eyebrows drawing together with sympathy. "...Well... Have you found someone like that?"

[Fumiko] "...I thought I had."

Fumiko's eyes brightened as she thought about the boy in question.

[Fumiko] "He is absolutely perfect. Kind-hearted, reliable, and sweet. He's also really good-looking. That Duchenne smile of his never misses..."

She tilted her head slightly, staring into the distance as she remembered him. Her admiration was still obvious, even through her sadness.

Fumio instinctively leaned back a little.

Perhaps it was jealousy.

Perhaps it was something else.

[Fumiko] "I've talked to him a few dozen times, and yet my ears have never gotten tired of hearing his voice. I already feel so safe around him..."

[Fumio] "...Uh... Okay. Understood..." Fumiko's smile suddenly faded as another memory returned to her.

"...I thought I was prepared enough. I expected the best possible scenario." Fumiko sighed. "I was too optimistic."
