Meanwhile, inside Nijigasaki's Student Council office...

Only two members of the council remained in the meeting room.

Concern and careful analysis filled the air as Student Council President Nana Nakagawa and Union Member Setsuna Yuki examined the incident report before them.

"I've never seen a student behave like this before..." Shioriko's vermilion eyes narrowed as she read the report. "By far, this is the worst case I've come across."

"Both Fumiko-san and I have been in contact with multiple students, kaichō," Nana explained. "I haven't found anyone who knows Williams personally. As for Fumiko-san, I believe she's still searching."

"If I were to gather information about him, I would ask Verde-san first. She seems rather naive about the situation, but I trust that she'll be truthful if I ask her about him." Shioriko picked up a notebook and began writing down several questions.

[Nana] "May I know if she's still on campus?"

[Shioriko] "I'm afraid her father has already taken her home."

"...Her father isn't aware of this?" Nana looked genuinely flabbergasted. Shioriko nodded.

"I didn't expect Verde-san to be so negligent... Being degraded in public didn't seem to bother her." Shioriko gently placed her pen down. "Tomorrow, I shall inform her father about this incident immediately."

The two students nodded in agreement, preparing to conclude the meeting.

Then came a gentle knock at the door.

"...? Come in! You may enter!" Nana called.

The door opened, revealing Fumiko accompanied by her newfound friend, Fumio, who lingered outside.

[Fumiko] "Excuse me. Was I interrupting something? Ah, you can come in too, Fumio."

Fumiko turned to signal him inside. Fumio responded with a shy grin.

However, as she turned back toward the council members, her tear-stained face became visible.

"...F-Fumiko-san?!" Nana was the first to notice. She hurriedly stood up. "Are those tears I see?! W-Were you crying? Did something happen?"

Hearing the word crying, Shioriko looked up with concern. "Is something the matter?"

"A-Ah, oh no..." Fumiko immediately covered her cheeks, trying to hide any remaining evidence of her breakdown. "I-I forgot... N-No, nothing is wrong! I'm perfectly fine!"

Fumio shook his head, clearly unconvinced. Shioriko noticed.

[Shioriko] "The student behind you begs to differ. Please tell us the truth..."

Fumiko shot Fumio a quick glare, frightening him slightly.

[Fumiko] "...Ngh... I-I'm sorry. I felt a little dejected a while ago, and it overwhelmed me."

"We're here for you, Fumiko-san!" Nana reassured her. "You can tell us anything!"

Nana's concern only made Fumiko more anxious about opening up. She gently tapped Fumio's arm, silently asking him to explain the situation instead.

"A-Atta—Ah... Eto..." Fumio struggled to get the words out. "Fumiko-san went through a tough rejection from my friend, Vaisu. She didn't take it too well, considering how much effort she put into it..."

Nana immediately sympathized. "Aww... I'm..so sorry to hear that…"

Shioriko, however, seemed to have something else in mind. "...I will be right back."

[Nana] "...Euh?"

Nana watched as Shioriko stood up and stormed out of the office.

[Nana] "E-Euh?! Mifune-san?! Where are you going?!"

[Fumiko] "Sh-Shio?!"

Fumiko watched Shioriko approach the door past her, with Fumio making way as Shioriko's pacing demanded that he move aside without saying a word.

[Fumiko] "D-Do you even know who he is?!"

"I do, and I'm quite disappointed." Shioriko's eyebrows drew together in unmistakable fury.

"SH-SHIO!! WAIT! YOU'VE MISUNDERSTOOD!!" Fumiko chased after her while Nana followed close behind.

[Fumio] "Oh Dear Lord..."

Fumio had no choice but to pursue the three of them.

[Fumio] "H-How the hell is she so fast despite only speed-walking?!"

Inside Nijigasaki's dance studio...

Two students remained in the room: a short girl with navy-blue hair and a boy with fluffy royal-purple hair.

Karin and Vyse were resting after their dance session, seated in front of the wall mirror and admiring their reflections.

"You're not drained from that, are you, Vyse-kun?" Karin brushed her side hair aside to get a better look at her dance partner.

"Hm? Nope, I'm perfectly fine." Vyse shook his head. "That was quite fun, Karin."

"Honestly, I expected to teach you a lot more in-depth, but you were a fast learner. You never told me you could dance, too…" Karin narrowed her eyes at him.

Vyse chuckled softly. "Hehe... You're not the first person I've done this with. Riku usually invites me to practice some of his routines."

"...Riku-kun dances?!" Karin slapped Vyse's shoulder before putting her hands on her hips. "You guys are keeping secrets from me!"

"A-Ah, I don't think he'd appreciate me saying that..." Vyse murmured an "oopsie" to himself as he stood up.

[Vyse] "Your routine was really different from his, though. All that spinning and... hip-swaying."

"Well, if you put it like that..." Karin rubbed one of the beauty marks on her chest, looking rather cheeky at Vyse's comment.

"Who am I kidding? It perfectly defines the Karin I know." The charismatic boy bent forward, his bangs hanging down as he flashed her a dazzling smile.

[Vyse] "I'm quite hungry. Come on, let's get something to eat. My treat."

[Karin] "...Dear God, this guy's a charmer…"

Karin struggled to hide the small blush spreading across her face.

She gently took the hand Vyse offered to help her stand. [Karin] "...Erm... Just us, right?"

"...What do you mean, just us?" Vyse tilted his head.

"N-No! Forget what I said!" Karin immediately abandoned the thought, grabbed her bag, and slung it over her shoulder.

"You're right, though. I'd kill for lunchtime in the café..." She reached for the doorknob and opened the door.

Then she stopped.

Standing on the other side was a stern-looking Shioriko Mifune.

Karin's heart nearly leaped out of her chest.

[Karin] "E-Euh? Seitokai...?"

Shioriko stared at Karin for a moment before turning toward her companion, who hummed in confusion.

"...You." Shioriko wasted no time. She marched toward Vyse, forcing him to step backward to avoid a collision.

[Shioriko] "How dare you hurt Sakura-san?!"

"Seitokai?! Vyse-kun?!" Karin moved aside, utterly confused about why her friend had suddenly become the target of the Student Council President's wrath.

"I do not care about your reputation! You have no right to disregard her appeal!" Shioriko aggressively poked Vyse in the chest. He could do little more than stare at her in bewilderment. "She only wanted your compliance!"

"U-Uhn! Mifune-san, is this really necessary...?" Vyse's furrowed eyebrows suggested that he had some idea what Shioriko was talking about.

Unfortunately, that only seemed to upset her further.

Before Shioriko could declare him a suspect, Fumiko and Nana came rushing into the room.

"SH-SHIO!! STOP!!" Fumiko hurriedly grabbed Shioriko's wrist to keep her away from Vyse, while Nana held her shoulders.

[Nana] "M-Mifune-san!! Vyse-san isn't a target!"

"T-Target?! Wh-What is going—" Karin stopped as she noticed Fumio panting in the doorway.

[Karin] "...Fumio-kun?"

[Fumio] "H-Hagh... H-Hey-." His voice cracked as he raised a hand to acknowledge her, still struggling to catch his breath.

Shioriko's eyebrows furrowed again, though her expression softened slightly as she looked toward her friends. "He hurt Fumiko-san!"

"Sh-She didn't mean that he rejected a request!" Nana stammered. "Sh-She meant..."

Nana glanced at Fumiko, hesitating before continuing. "...He rejected her confession."

"...Confession?" Shioriko looked at Vyse, who had averted his gaze out of respect and uneasiness. She then turned toward Fumiko, who was looking away out of embarrassment.

[Shioriko] "...I don't quite understand."

Nana noticed the shame on both faces and decided to whisper into Shioriko's ear, rising onto her tiptoes to reach it.

[Nana] "Sh-She confessed her love, Mifune-san..."

"...Love." The word lingered in Shioriko's mind for a moment.

She then faced Vyse and Fumiko.

[Shioriko] "I don't fully understand the concept, but I do know that it doesn't concern me. I apologize for any inconvenience I have caused."

Shioriko gently bowed her head to the two students.

Vyse quietly nodded in acknowledgment, while Fumiko returned the gesture with a hint of anxiety.

"...V-Vyse-kun has an admirer...?" Karin stared at her friend in disbelief, her mouth hanging open as she witnessed Fumiko's obvious admiration.

[Fumiko] "...!!" Seeing Karin's reaction, Fumiko suddenly wondered whether she was the person Vyse had feelings for.

She wanted to disappear entirely, along with all the regret and embarrassment she was feeling.

[Fumio] "...Oh, God."

Fumio's pity for Fumiko was off the charts. Knowing the full context of the situation was enough to make his heart ache for her.

It also meant he had an opportunity to intervene before everyone embarrassed themselves any further.

[Fumio] "...Ah, so that's that. Just a little misunderstanding."

Everyone turned toward Fumio as he approached.

Vyse was the only one who seemed genuinely shocked to see him.

"...Fumio?" His eyes widened more than they had at any point during the confrontation.

[Fumio] "I'm sure everyone understands, right? It's best that we forget this ever happened and head home to rest. Besides, the Student Council definitely needs a break after all its business, right?"

Fumio addressed everyone in the room before turning toward Fumiko, whose expression brightened at his encouragement.

"...You are right," Shioriko agreed. "You should all be heading home by now. No student should remain on school grounds too late."

"Ah, that's right. My parents might be looking for me. The same probably applies to all of you," Nana added.

"...Y-Yeah." Fumiko couldn't quite manage a proper sentence. She kept looking into Fumio's eyes, clearly grateful for his gesture.

Karin walked beside Vyse and Fumio, still clutching her bag. "I still don't know what exactly just happened, but... please take care, all of you."

"Thank you! The same applies to you as well!" Nana smiled, with Shioriko nodding beside her.

Then Nana noticed Fumiko walking toward the maroon-haired boy.

[Fumio] "...Hmm?"

Fumiko held her hands together in front of her and offered Fumio the sweetest smile anyone had seen from her that day.

[Fumiko] "I appreciate what you've done. Not just right now, but a while ago as well."

Fumio's face turned red again.

He couldn't comprehend how someone so beautiful could be standing right in front of him, smiling at him of all people.

Fumiko blinked and giggled. "I want to see you again. There really should be more boys like you out there."

With one last grin, she brushed her side hair aside.

[Fumiko] "I'm looking forward to seeing you again next week, Fumio-kun."

[Fumio] "...H...Ha—ha~"

Fumio's limbs creaked and steamed like those of a malfunctioning robot. He took a moment to compose himself before responding.

[Fumio] "Ahem! Of course, of course... I'll see you as well, Sakura-san."

[Fumiko] "Oh, please call me Fumiko. You've earned it."

[Fumio] "A-Ah! I-I suppose... Have a nice day, F-Fu..."

Fumio lowered his eyes toward his toes, unable to hold her gaze.

[Fumio] "...'Miko-chan."

He could no longer contain his blush. Behind him, Karin found the entire situation hilarious.

[Fumiko] "...'Miko-chan... I like that~."

Fumiko giggled, covering her lips with her fingers. She then waved at Fumio and the others. [Fumiko] "See you then, 'Mio-kun!"

Before leaving with her fellow Student Council members, Fumiko gave one last smiling glance toward Vyse and Karin.

Fumio remained where he stood with his two friends, his heart still thumping away.

"...I'm quite surprised, Fumio." Vyse looked impressed by his friend's sudden willingness to interact with other people.

[Vyse] "I never expected you to finally talk to her."

"That finally came full circle, hasn't it?" Karin excitedly patted Fumio's shoulder. "I'd be dying to hear your side of the story~."

"I-uh... M-Maybe... Maybe I'll save it for another time..." Fumio walked sluggishly, his head lowered as he tried to calm his blushing face.

Then, a sudden thought energized him back to his usual self.

[Fumio] "Teh! We're gonna discuss the fact that you rejected her confession, though!"

"U-Uhn! Y-You know about that?" Vyse's smug smile disappeared, replaced by a frown.

"Oh, that much is apparent..." Karin shot Vyse a glare, apparently displeased by the conversation.

"Grh! It should've been me..." Fumio clutched his head, imagining what he'd give to be in Vyse's position.

[Fumio] "She even made a whole song about it! I would've loved to see that..." He turned toward Vyse, his curiosity getting the better of him. [Fumio] "How did it go?"

[Vyse] "I-I didn't know how to respond..."

Vyse recalled the moment he'd spent twiddling his fingers, sweat gathering on his forehead as Fumiko performed.

[Vyse] "While she was singing, I kept trying to figure out what to say at the end..."

"Were you even listening to the song?!" Fumio threw his hands up. "She worked hard on it, you know!"

"O-Of course I was! I respect a piece of art! I just... didn't expect it to cross my to-do list today..." Vyse protested, while Karin quietly sighed in relief.

"...Oh my, Vyse-kun~. You offend me. You're apparently a heartbreaker now?" Karin placed one hand on her hip and the other against her chest.

"I-I really didn't mean it! Besides, it's weird for me, considering I know Fumio's had his eyes on her for so long..." Vyse patted Fumio's back.

Fumio could only nod from side to side. "Oh well. That's enough surprises for one day. Come, let's go grab ourselves some lunch~."

Karin happily opened the door. The two boys nodded and followed her outside.

The trio of friends walked along Nijigasaki's bridge when a familiar voice suddenly called out to them.

[Alicia] "Ya-hoooo!!~ Wait for me, buddies!!~"

An energetic girl came dashing toward them. [Alicia] "Glad I could catch up with you lot! The volleyball team needed my help the whole day, so that's a thing—"

She skidded to a stop in front of the three, braking safely before she could crash into anyone.

"Ah, Ali-chan... There you are~. Right in the nick of—" Karin stopped mid-sentence when she noticed that one member of the group wasn't particularly happy to see Alicia.

Fumio's eyes had darkened as he stared directly at his cousin.

Alicia's smile began to falter under his gaze.

[Fumio] "...You and Fumiko."

[Alicia] "...Euh-"

[Fumio] "You and Fumiko were talking, and you DIDN'T TELL ME."

Alicia nervously smiled, her eyes darting from side to side as she struggled to respond.

[Alicia] "G-Guh- I-I was just, uh... checking out the cheerleaders! And I saw her there and thought damn girl you are fine-"

"FUMIKO'S A CHEERLEADER AND YOU NEVER BOTHERED SHOWING ME?!" Fumio's hands curled into claws, frightening Alicia half to death.

"E-Euh?! I-Isn't that a weird thing to do? To picture her up close?!"

Her question made Fumio return to a neutral expression.

He paused, considered it, and finally nodded.

[Fumio] "...Yeah. You're right. That is a bit weird."
