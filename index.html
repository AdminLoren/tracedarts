Children of the Authors

Synopsis

Entities known as the Author Gods once bestowed extraordinary gifts upon young teenagers, enlisting them as their personal agents and prophets. After enduring years of agonizing trials, heavy losses, and the grueling weight of their duties, these Authors earned their peace. They settled down, built lives, and raised a new generation—hoping to shield their offspring from the hardships they once faced.

Unfortunately, that fragile peace was short-lived. Recognizing inevitable looming threats, the Author Gods identified immense latent potential in the Authors' young children. Despite fierce protests from the veteran Authors—desperate to keep their families out of the fray—the day ultimately arrived when the gifts had to be passed down. To survive a world where their bloodline makes them prime targets, the new generation must now inherit the mantle of Authors, rising not only to protect the realms, but to defend their very existence.

Origins

Began nearly eight years ago as a collaborative storytelling and impromptu scriptwriting project. Over time, what started as simple, playful ideas gradually matured into a deeply developed universe defined by distinct character chemistry, generational lore, and influences from beloved anime and classic gaming franchises.

As the project evolved into a solo passion, the focus shifted toward bringing these long-standing characters to life visually. Through dedicated digital illustration, reference matching, and asset production, each character was given a distinct visual identity for the first time since its conception.

This web encyclopedia serves as an interactive home for that eight-year journey—combining custom character renders, relationship mappings, and structural data into a cohesive archive.

Note: This project was built purely for fun as a passion project. I am not a professional artist, just someone learning and creating out of love for these characters.
